Our Story

From a Secondhand Roaster

Driftwood began in the spring of nineteen twelve with a secondhand drum roaster, a borrowed counter, and one stubborn conviction: that a neighborhood deserves coffee made with patience. Four generations later, the drum still turns each morning before the lamps are lit out front.

We buy from six farms we know by name, roast in batches small enough to taste, and pour every cup as if the person across the counter had come a long way to sit exactly there.

“Drink your coffee slowly, and with reverence.” House Rule No. 1
